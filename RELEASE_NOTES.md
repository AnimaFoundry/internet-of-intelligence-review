# Review preprint v1.0.0

Initial English review: **Low-Power Sensing for an Internet of Intelligence: Materials, Distributed Architectures, and World-Model Learning**.

The release includes the PDF and editable manuscript, 49 cited sources with reading-scope metadata, representative search and selection records, five comparison tables, an original schematic, and reproducible hypothetical budget arithmetic. The final substantive section preserves AnimaFoundry's research vision as a testable agenda.

This is a critical integrative review, not a systematic review, peer-reviewed article, experimentally demonstrated platform, or invention disclosure. Source experiments were not independently reproduced. The synthetic numerical examples establish arithmetic only. Internal review corrected system-boundary, sample-count, statistical interpretation, and evidence-strength issues before publication.
