The final substantive section preserves this vision in academic language. The text, comparison tables, schematic, and arithmetic scripts were newly written for this review with OpenAI Codex assistance. AI assistance and internal agent checks are disclosed in the manuscript; they are not independent human peer review.

The work offers a critical synthesis and prospective research agenda. No institutional affiliation, grant, external endorsement, DOI, experimental measurement, or peer-review outcome is invented.
